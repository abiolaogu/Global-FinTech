# Individual Users

## Getting Started

### 1. Registration & KYC

#### Step 1: Create Account
1. Download AtlasX app (iOS/Android) or visit https://app.atlasx.io
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter email and create strong password (min 12 characters)
4. Verify email address

#### Step 2: Complete KYC Verification
**Why?** Regulatory requirement for financial services

**Documents Needed:**
- Government-issued ID (passport, driver's license, or national ID)
- Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement <3 months old)
- Selfie for liveness detection

**Process:**
1. Navigate to Profile → Verification
2. Upload ID document
3. Take selfie following on-screen instructions
4. Upload proof of address
5. Wait 10-30 minutes for automated verification
6. If flagged, manual review within 24 hours

**Verification Levels:**
| Level | Limits | Requirements |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Unverified | $0 (view only) | Email only |
| Basic | $1,000/month | ID verification |
| Full | Unlimited | ID + Address + Selfie |

---

### 2. Setting Up Your Account

#### Enable Biometric Authentication
1. Go to Settings → Security
2. Tap "Enable Biometric Login"
3. Authenticate with Face ID/Touch ID/Fingerprint
4. Your login token is encrypted and stored securely

**Benefits:**
- Login in < 2 seconds
- No password needed
- More secure than password

#### Set Up Multi-Currency Wallets
1. Navigate to Wallets
2. Tap "Add Wallet"
3. Select currency (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, etc.)
4. Wallet created instantly

**Supported Currencies:** 25+ fiat currencies, 10+ cryptocurrencies

#### Connect Your Bank Account (Open Banking)
1. Go to Wallets → Fund
2. Select "Connect Bank Account"
3. Choose your bank from the list
4. Authenticate with your bank login
5. Select accounts to link
6. Grant AtlasX read access (90-day consent)

**What AtlasX Can See:**
- ✅ Account balances
- ✅ Transaction history
- ✅ Account holder information
- ❌ Cannot move money without your explicit authorization
- ❌ Cannot see passwords

**What This Enables:**
- Instant wallet funding
- Automatic categorization of spending
- Credit assessment for P2P lending

---

### 3. Using Your Wallets

#### Fund Your Wallet

**Method 1: Bank Transfer (Open Banking)**
1. Wallets → Select wallet → Fund
2. Choose connected bank account
3. Enter amount
4. Authenticate with biometric/password
5. Funds arrive instantly

**Method 2: Debit/Credit Card**
1. Wallets → Select wallet → Fund
2. Choose "Card"
3. Enter card details (saved securely for future)
4. Enter amount
5. Funds arrive in 1-3 minutes
6. Fee: 2.9% + $0.30

**Method 3: Wire Transfer**
1. Wallets → Select wallet → Fund
2. Choose "Wire Transfer"
3. Copy account details
4. Initiate wire from your bank
5. Funds arrive in 1-2 business days
6. No fee

#### Withdraw Funds
1. Wallets → Select wallet → Withdraw
2. Choose destination (bank account or card)
3. Enter amount
4. Authenticate with biometric
5. Processing: 1-2 business days
6. Fee: Free (first 3/month for Silver+), $2 thereafter

# Borrowers

## P2P Lending: Borrowing Guide

### 1. Eligibility

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Full KYC verification
- ✅ Age 18+ (21+ in some jurisdictions)
- ✅ Connected bank account (for credit assessment)
- ✅ Credit score available (or equivalent)

**Credit Score Tiers:**
| Tier | Score Range | Max Interest Rate |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Excellent | 750+ | 8% APR |
| Good | 700-749 | 12% APR |
| Fair | 650-699 | 18% APR |
| Poor | <650 | 25% APR |

---

### 2. Creating a Loan Listing

#### Step 1: Start Application
1. Navigate to Borrow
2. Tap "Request Loan"
3. Answer questions:
   - Loan amount ($1,000-$50,000)
   - Purpose (debt consolidation, home improvement, business, etc.)
   - Term (12, 24, 36, or 60 months)

#### Step 2: Credit Assessment
1. Connect bank account (via Open Banking)
2. AtlasX analyzes:
   - Income (direct deposits)
   - Spending patterns
   - Existing debts
   - Account age
3. Receive instant credit decision

#### Step 3: Review Offer
AtlasX provides:
- **Maximum loan amount:** Based on creditworthiness
- **Interest rate:** Within tier cap
- **Monthly payment:** Fixed amount
- **Total interest:** Over life of loan
- **APR:** All-in rate including fees

**Example Offer:**
```
Loan Amount: $10,000
Interest Rate: 10% APR
Term: 36 months
Monthly Payment: $322.67
Total Interest: $1,616.12
Total Repayment: $11,616.12
```

#### Step 4: Submit Listing
1. Review and accept terms
2. Listing goes live on marketplace
3. Lenders can browse and invest
4. Funding window: 30 days

---

### 3. Funding Process

**Your listing is visible to thousands of lenders.**

**Status Updates:**
- **0-25% funded:** "Seeking Funding"
- **25-75% funded:** "Partially Funded"
- **75-99% funded:** "Nearly Funded"
- **100% funded:** "Funded - Disbursing"

**What Lenders See:**
- ✅ Loan amount, rate, term
- ✅ Purpose
- ✅ Credit tier (excellent/good/fair/poor)
- ✅ Employment status
- ❌ Name, address, or personal details

**Funding Timeline:**
- Average time to fund: 3-7 days
- If not funded in 30 days: Listing expires (no fee)

---

### 4. Loan Disbursement

Once 100% funded:
1. **Verification Call:** 5-minute call to confirm identity (anti-fraud)
2. **Final Acceptance:** Sign loan agreement electronically
3. **Disbursement:** Funds arrive in your AtlasX wallet within 1 business day
4. **Withdrawal:** Transfer to your bank account (free)

**First Payment Due:** 30 days from disbursement

---

### 5. Repayment

#### Automatic Payments (Recommended)
1. Borrow → Active Loans → Setup Auto-Pay
2. Link bank account or AtlasX wallet
3. Payment deducted automatically on due date
4. Never miss a payment

#### Manual Payments
1. Borrow → Active Loans → Make Payment
2. Choose wallet or payment method
3. Enter amount (minimum = monthly payment)
4. Confirm

**Early Repayment:** No prepayment penalty. Pay off anytime and save on interest.

**Late Payments:**
- 1-15 days late: $25 late fee
- 15+ days late: Additional $15/week
- 30+ days late: Reported to credit bureaus
- 60+ days late: Loan marked as defaulted

---

### 6. Managing Your Loan

**View Loan Details:**
1. Borrow → Active Loans → Select loan
2. See:
   - Remaining balance
   - Next payment due
   - Payment history
   - Lenders (anonymized list)
   - Payoff amount

**Request Modification:**
If facing hardship:
1. Borrow → Active Loans → Request Hardship
2. Explain situation
3. AtlasX reviews within 48 hours
4. Possible modifications:
   - Temporary reduced payments
   - Extended term
   - Grace period (1-2 months)

---

### 7. Improving Your Chances of Funding

**Tips:**
- ✅ **Connect bank account:** Lenders prefer verified income
- ✅ **Detailed purpose:** "Home renovation" better than "Personal"
- ✅ **Lower amount:** $5,000 funds faster than $50,000
- ✅ **Shorter term:** 24 months preferred over 60 months
- ✅ **Higher tier:** Work on credit score before applying

# Lenders/Investors

## P2P Lending: Investing Guide

### 1. Getting Started as a Lender

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Full KYC verification
- ✅ Accredited investor status (in some jurisdictions)
- ✅ Funding source (bank account or AtlasX wallet)
- ✅ Acknowledge risks

**Minimum Investment:** $25 per loan

---

### 2. Understanding Risk & Return

**Historical Returns by Tier (Illustrative):**
| Risk Tier | Interest Rate Range | Default Rate | Net Return |
|-----------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Excellent | 6-8% | 1% | 5-7% |
| Good | 10-12% | 3% | 7-9% |
| Fair | 15-18% | 8% | 7-10% |
| Poor | 20-25% | 15% | 5-10% |

**Diversification is Key:**
- ❌ Don't invest $10,000 in one loan
- ✅ Invest $25-$100 across 100-400 loans

---

### 3. Browsing Loan Marketplace

1. Navigate to Invest
2. Browse listings by:
   - **Risk Tier:** Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
   - **Interest Rate:** High to Low
   - **Funding Progress:** Nearly funded → Just listed
   - **Loan Purpose:** Debt consolidation, business, etc.

**Listing Details:**
- Credit tier
- Interest rate (your return)
- Loan amount
- Term length
- Purpose
- Employment status
- Funding progress

**Filters:**
1. Tap "Filters"
2. Set criteria:
   - Min interest rate: 10%
   - Max loan amount: $5,000
   - Risk tier: Good or Excellent only
   - Minimum credit score (if available)

---

### 4. Investing in Loans

#### Manual Investment
1. Browse marketplace
2. Tap on listing
3. Review details
4. Tap "Invest"
5. Enter amount ($25-$10,000 per loan)
6. Confirm
7. Funds reserved from wallet

#### Auto-Invest (Recommended for Diversification)
1. Invest → Auto-Invest → Setup
2. Set criteria:
   - **Monthly investment:** $500-$10,000
   - **Per-loan amount:** $25-$100
   - **Risk tiers:** Select tiers to invest in
   - **Minimum interest rate:** e.g., 8%
3. Enable Auto-Invest
4. AtlasX automatically invests across 20-200 loans monthly

**Example Auto-Invest Portfolio:**
```
Monthly Budget: $1,000
Per-Loan Investment: $50
Result: 20 loans funded automatically
```

**Diversification Breakdown:**
- 40% Excellent tier (6-8% APR)
- 30% Good tier (10-12% APR)
- 20% Fair tier (15-18% APR)
- 10% Poor tier (20-25% APR)

---

### 5. Receiving Repayments

**Monthly Payments:**
1. Borrowers make payments → Platform distributes proportionally to lenders
2. Payments arrive in your AtlasX wallet
3. Option 1: Reinvest automatically
4. Option 2: Withdraw to bank account

**Example:**
```
Your Investment: $1,000 (10% of $10,000 loan)
Borrower Payment: $322.67/month
Your Share: $32.27/month
Annual Return: ~10% APR
```

---

### 6. Portfolio Management

**View Portfolio:**
1. Invest → My Investments
2. See:
   - Total invested
   - Outstanding principal
   - Total returns (interest earned)
   - Active loans
   - Completed loans
   - Defaulted loans

**Performance Metrics:**
- **Net Annual Return:** Total returns - defaults / principal
- **Default Rate:** % of loans that defaulted
- **Weighted Avg Interest Rate:** Portfolio average rate

**Reinvestment Settings:**
1. Invest → Settings → Reinvest
2. Options:
   - Reinvest all payments
   - Reinvest principal only (withdraw interest)
   - Withdraw all payments

---

### 7. Risk Management

**Defaults:**
- If borrower defaults, you lose principal + unpaid interest
- AtlasX attempts collection (no fee to you)
- Recovered funds distributed to lenders

**Late Payments:**
- Borrower misses payment → Marked as late
- AtlasX contacts borrower (email, phone, SMS)
- Late fees added to borrower balance (benefits lenders)
- If 60+ days late → Loan marked default

**Protection:**
- ❌ No FDIC insurance (P2P loans are not deposits)
- ❌ No guaranteed returns
- ✅ Diversification reduces risk

---

### 8. Tax Reporting

**Tax Treatment (US):**
- Interest income = Ordinary income
- Reported on 1099-INT (if >$10/year)
- Capital losses from defaults may be deductible

**Year-End Statement:**
1. Invest → Tax Documents
2. Download 1099-INT (January)
3. Consult tax professional

```

2. Use in Authorization header:

3. Rotate keys every 90 days
```

### 4. Webhooks

**Setup:**
1. Developer Portal → Webhooks
2. Add endpoint URL: `https://your-app.com/webhooks/atlasx`
3. Choose events to subscribe to
4. Receive webhook secret

**Verify Signature:**
```python
import hmac
import hashlib

def verify_webhook(payload, signature, secret):
    expected = hmac.new(
        secret.encode(),
        payload.encode(),
        hashlib.sha256
    ).hexdigest()
    return hmac.compare_digest(signature, expected)
```

**Events:**
- `transaction.created`
- `transaction.completed`
- `payment.succeeded`
- `payment.failed`
- `loan.funded`
- `wallet.balance_updated`

# Compliance Officers

## Compliance Dashboard

### 1. AML Monitoring

**Daily Workflow:**
1. Login → Compliance Dashboard
2. Check alerts:
   - Sanctions matches (URGENT)
   - Structuring detected
   - High-value transactions
   - Unusual patterns
3. Review and action each alert

**Sanctions Screening:**
- Automatic screening on:
  - User registration
  - Every transaction
  - Monthly batch re-screening
- Lists: OFAC SDN, EU, UN, HMT

**Structuring Detection:**
- Multiple transactions <$10,000 in 24 hours
- Pattern: $9,500 × 3 = ALERT

---

### 2. SAR Filing

**When to File SAR:**
- Confirmed suspicious activity
- Transactions ≥$5,000 with known or suspected criminal activity
- Structuring
- Terrorist financing indicators

**Process:**
1. Compliance → SARs → File New
2. Complete form:
   - Subject information
   - Suspicious activity description
   - Supporting documents
3. Review with legal
4. Submit to FinCEN (US) or FCA (UK)
5. **DO NOT** notify customer (tipping off = crime)

**Deadline:** 30 days from detection

---

### 3. Transaction Reporting

**CTR (Currency Transaction Report) - US:**
- File for cash transactions >$10,000
- Aggregate related transactions
- Due within 15 days

**EMIR Reporting - EU:**
- Report derivatives transactions
- Daily submission
