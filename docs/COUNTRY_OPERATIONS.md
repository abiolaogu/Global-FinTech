## Overview

Global FinTech operates in **60+ countries** across Africa, Americas, Asia-Pacific, and Europe. This document provides detailed operations models, organizational structures, and regulatory requirements for each major market.

### Geographic Presence

**Tier 1 Markets (Full Operations):**
- Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa (Africa)
- USA, Brazil, Mexico (Americas)
- Philippines, India, Indonesia (Asia-Pacific)

**Tier 2 Markets (Limited Operations):**
- 15 additional African countries
- 10 additional Asian countries
- 5 European countries

**Tier 3 Markets (Partnership Model):**
- 25+ countries via partnerships

### Operating Models

**Model A: Full Stack**
- Complete operations team
- Local entity established
- All licenses held locally
- Example: Nigeria, Kenya, USA

**Model B: Hybrid**
- Core team locally
- Shared services from regional hub
- Mix of local and regional licenses
- Example: Ghana, Philippines

**Model C: Partnership**
- Minimal local presence
- Partner handles operations
- White-label or integration
- Example: Smaller markets

## Expansion Playbook

### New Market Entry Process

**Phase 1: Market Assessment (4-6 weeks)**

1. **Market Research**
   - Population, GDP, smartphone penetration
   - Existing financial infrastructure
   - Competitive landscape
   - Remittance flows
   - Regulatory environment

2. **Regulatory Feasibility**
   - License requirements
   - Capital requirements
   - Timeline to license
   - Ongoing compliance burden
   - Local entity requirements

3. **Go/No-Go Decision**
   - Market size ≥ 10M population (Tier 1)
   - Smartphone penetration ≥ 40%
   - Reasonable regulatory path (< 18 months)
   - Capital requirement < $5M
   - Strategic fit (regional expansion, remittance corridors)

**Phase 2: Market Entry (6-18 months)**

1. **Legal Entity Formation (Month 1-2)**
   - Engage local counsel
   - Register local entity
   - Open bank accounts
   - Tax registration

2. **License Application (Month 2-18)**
   - Prepare application materials
   - Submit to regulator
   - Respond to questions
   - Site visits/interviews
   - Receive approval

3. **Infrastructure Setup (Month 6-12)**
   - Office lease
   - Hire country manager
   - Recruit core team
   - IT infrastructure
   - Partner integrations

4. **Product Localization (Month 8-12)**
   - Language translation
   - Currency support
   - Local payment rails
   - Compliance features (local KYC rules)

**Phase 3: Launch (Month 12-18)**

1. **Soft Launch**
   - Employee beta
   - Friends & family
   - 1,000 user pilot

2. **Public Launch**
   - Marketing campaign
   - PR push
   - Referral incentives
   - Partnership announcements

**Phase 4: Scale (Month 18+)**

1. **Growth**
   - User acquisition
   - Feature expansion
   - Partnership growth

2. **Optimization**
   - Unit economics
   - Operational efficiency
   - Path to profitability

### Country Selection Criteria

**Tier 1 Market Criteria (Full Operations):**
- Population: >20M
- GDP per capita: >$1,500
- Smartphone penetration: >50%
- Regulatory: Established fintech framework
- Competition: Market leader has <40% share
- Strategic: Remittance corridor or regional hub

**Examples:** Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Brazil, Mexico, Philippines, Indonesia

**Tier 2 Market Criteria (Hybrid):**
- Population: 5-20M
- GDP per capita: >$1,000
- Smartphone penetration: >40%
- Regulatory: Clear path to license
- Strategic: Regional expansion, diaspora markets

**Examples:** Ghana, Uganda, Tanzania, Senegal, Ivory Coast

**Tier 3 Market Criteria (Partnership):**
- Population: <5M or established player dominates
- Regulatory: Complex or restrictive
- Entry mode: White-label partnership or integration

**Examples:** Smaller markets, India (RBI restrictions favor partnership)
