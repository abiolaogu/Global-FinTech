## Company Overview

### Who We Are

AtlasX is a global fintech platform revolutionizing financial services across 60+ countries. We provide:

- **Digital Wallets** - Multi-currency wallets for individuals and businesses
- **Payment Rails** - Integration with 46+ payment methods globally
- **Investment Platform** - Access to stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and alternative investments
- **ROSCA (Savings Circles)** - Digital rotating savings and credit associations
- **P2P Lending** - Peer-to-peer lending marketplace
- **Open Banking** - Bank account aggregation and payment initiation
- **AI Chat Assistant** - Natural language interface for all platform operations

### Our Reach

**Geographic Presence:**
- **Africa:** 20+ countries (Nigeria, Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, Egypt, Tanzania, etc.)
- **Americas:** USA, Canada, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, etc.
- **Asia-Pacific:** India, Philippines, Thailand, Singapore, Indonesia, Vietnam, etc.
- **Europe:** UK, Germany, France (expanding)

**User Base:** 1.5M+ users (target: 75M by Year 5)
**Transaction Volume:** $180M+ GMV annually (target: $15B by Year 5)
**Team Size:** 150+ employees across 15 countries

## Employment Policies

### Equal Opportunity

AtlasX is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on:
- Race, color, ethnicity, or national origin
- Gender identity or sexual orientation
- Age or disability
- Religion or political beliefs
- Marital or family status
- Any other protected characteristic

### Employment Types

#### Full-Time Employees
- 40 hours/week standard
- Eligible for all benefits
- Annual performance reviews
- Career development opportunities

#### Part-Time Employees
- 20-30 hours/week
- Prorated benefits
- Flexible scheduling
- Opportunity to transition to full-time

#### Contractors
- Project-based or ongoing
- Separate contractor agreement
- Not eligible for employee benefits
- May transition to employee status

### Work Schedule

**Standard Hours:**
- Monday - Friday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (local time)
- 1-hour lunch break
- Flexible start time (8:00 AM - 10:00 AM)

**Remote Work:**
- Hybrid model: 3 days office, 2 days remote (varies by role)
- Fully remote for certain positions
- Must maintain availability during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local)

**Time Off:**
- Submit requests 2 weeks in advance
- Manager approval required
- Emergency leave: notify immediately

### Attendance

**Expected:**
- Punctuality for meetings
- Communication if running late
- Notify manager of absences
- Track time accurately (for hourly employees)

**Unplanned Absences:**
1. Notify manager ASAP (call, don't email)
2. Provide estimated return date
3. Delegate urgent tasks
4. Submit documentation (medical certificate if sick > 3 days)

## Security & Compliance

### Information Security

#### Password Policy
- Minimum 12 characters
- Mix of upper, lower, numbers, symbols
- Change every 90 days
- No reuse of last 5 passwords
- Use password manager (1Password provided)

#### Device Security
- Full disk encryption required
- Auto-lock after 5 minutes
- Biometric authentication enabled
- Install updates within 7 days
- Report lost/stolen devices immediately

#### Data Handling

**Classification Levels:**
1. **Public:** Marketing materials, job postings
2. **Internal:** Policies, general business docs
3. **Confidential:** User data, financial reports
4. **Restricted:** Source code, encryption keys

**Handling Rules:**
| Level | Storage | Sharing | Encryption |
|-------|---------|---------|------------|
| Public | Any | Public | Optional |
| Internal | Company systems | Internal only | TLS |
| Confidential | Encrypted storage | Need-to-know | AES-256 |
| Restricted | HSM/Vault | Authorized only | AES-256 |

### Regulatory Compliance

All employees must complete:
- **Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Training** - Annual
- **Data Privacy (GDPR/NDPR) Training** - Annual
- **Information Security Awareness** - Quarterly
- **Role-Specific Compliance Training** - As required

**Compliance Responsibilities:**
- Know your role's requirements
- Report suspicious activity
- Follow KYC procedures
- Maintain audit trails
- Cooperate with audits

### Incident Reporting

**Security Incidents:**
1. Report immediately to security@atlasx.io
2. Don't try to fix yourself
3. Preserve evidence
4. Follow instructions from Security team

**Compliance Violations:**
1. Report to compliance@atlasx.io or anonymous hotline
2. No retaliation for good-faith reports
3. Investigations are confidential
4. Violations may result in termination

## Performance Management

### Review Cycle

**Quarterly Check-ins (Informal):**
- 30-minute 1-on-1 with manager
- Discuss progress, challenges, goals
- No formal rating

**Annual Review (Formal):**
- Self-assessment
- Manager assessment
- 360-degree feedback (for managers)
- Performance rating (1-5 scale)
- Compensation discussion

**Rating Scale:**
1. **Does Not Meet Expectations** - Performance improvement plan required
2. **Partially Meets Expectations** - Some areas need improvement
3. **Meets Expectations** - Solid performance
4. **Exceeds Expectations** - Consistently goes above and beyond
5. **Outstanding** - Top 5% of company

### Goal Setting (OKRs)

**Objectives and Key Results:**
- Set quarterly OKRs
- Align with company/team goals
- 3-5 objectives, 3-4 key results each
- Track progress weekly
- Review in 1-on-1s

**Example OKR:**
- **Objective:** Improve payment success rate
- **Key Results:**
  - KR1: Reduce payment failures from 5% to 2%
  - KR2: Implement retry logic for 3 major payment rails
  - KR3: Achieve 99.5% uptime for payment API

### Performance Improvement

**When Needed:**
- Rating of 1 or 2
- Consistent missed deadlines
- Quality issues
- Behavioral problems

**Process:**
1. Meeting with manager to discuss concerns
2. 30/60/90-day improvement plan
3. Weekly check-ins
4. Resources/support provided
5. Re-evaluation at end of period

**Outcomes:**
- Improvement shown → Continue employment
- No improvement → Termination

## Offboarding

### Resignation

**Notice Period:**
- Individual contributors: 2 weeks
- Managers: 4 weeks
- Senior leadership: 8 weeks

**Exit Process:**
1. Submit resignation letter to manager
2. Manager informs HR
3. Knowledge transfer plan created
4. Exit interview scheduled
5. Return company property
6. Final paycheck + unused PTO

### Retirement

**Eligibility:**
- Age 60+ with 5+ years service
- Age 65+ with any service

**Retirement Benefits:**
- Pension as per local regulations
- Option to continue health insurance (at own cost)
- Lifetime discount: 50% off platform fees

### Termination

**With Cause:**
- Gross misconduct
- Repeated policy violations
- Poor performance (after PIP)
- Immediate termination, no severance

**Without Cause (Layoff):**
- Business reasons (restructuring, downsizing)
- 30-day notice or pay in lieu
- Severance: 1 month salary per year of service (minimum 2 months)
- Benefits continue for 3 months
- Outplacement support
