# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

This document outlines the product requirements for the global fintech platform.

## 1. Introduction

The Global Fintech Platform is a multi-currency, multi-asset financial application designed to provide a seamless and comprehensive suite of services, including digital banking, cryptocurrency trading, and real-time cross-border payments. The platform aims to serve a global audience, from individual retail customers to large corporations, with a focus on security, regulatory compliance, and user experience.

## 2. User Personas

This section details the key user personas the platform is designed to serve.

### 2.1. "Digital Nomad" Diana (Digital-Native Millennial)
- **Age:** 28
- **Occupation:** Freelance UX Designer
- **Needs:** A single platform to manage multiple currencies, receive payments from international clients, and easily convert between fiat and crypto. Values low fees and a mobile-first experience.
- **Goals:** To travel the world without worrying about banking logistics or high FX fees.

### 2.2. "World Traveler" William (International Traveler)
- **Age:** 45
- **Occupation:** Sales Director
- **Needs:** A reliable way to make payments in different countries without carrying multiple cards. Needs real-time FX rates and the ability to hold balances in several currencies.
- **Goals:** To avoid hefty card fees and poor exchange rates while traveling for business and leisure.

### 2.3. "Crypto Chris" (Crypto Enthusiast)
- **Age:** 24
- **Occupation:** Software Developer
- **Needs:** A secure and regulated platform to buy, sell, and hold a variety of cryptocurrencies. Wants to easily move funds between their fiat and crypto wallets and use crypto for payments.
- **Goals:** To participate in the digital asset economy through a trusted and feature-rich application.

### 2.4. "Small Business" Sarah (Small Business Owner)
- **Age:** 35
- **Occupation:** E-commerce Entrepreneur
- **Needs:** A business account that can handle international payments from customers and suppliers. Requires tools for expense management, corporate cards for employees, and simple integration with accounting software.
- **Goals:** To scale her business globally by simplifying cross-border finance.

### 2.5. "Underbanked" Ugo (Unbanked/Underbanked Population)
- **Age:** 30
- **Occupation:** Gig Economy Worker
- **Needs:** Access to basic financial services like a digital wallet, P2P transfers, and a way to receive payments without a traditional bank account. Relies on mobile access and low-cost transactions.
- **Goals:** To achieve financial inclusion and security through an accessible and affordable digital platform.

## 3. User Stories

## 4. Feature Specifications

This section outlines the core features of the platform.

### 4.1. Core Banking & Payments
- **User Onboarding:** Seamless, mobile-first registration and identity verification.
- **KYC/AML:** Automated document verification (OCR) and sanctions screening.
- **Digital Wallets:** Create and manage wallets for 50+ fiat currencies.
- **Fund Transfers:** Real-time domestic (ACH/SEPA) and international (SWIFT) transfers.
- **P2P Payments:** Instant peer-to-peer transfers within the platform ecosystem.
- **Card Issuance:** Issue, manage, and control virtual and physical debit cards (Visa/Mastercard).

### 4.2. Cryptocurrency & Digital Assets
- **Crypto Wallets:** Securely hold, send, and receive Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, and ERC-20 tokens.
- **Crypto Trading:** In-app trading with real-time price feeds from Chainlink oracles.
- **Cross-Chain Bridges:** Interoperability with Polygon, Stellar, and Ripple networks.

### 4.3. Business & Corporate Accounts
- **Business Onboarding (KYB):** A streamlined process for verifying business entities.
- **Multi-User Access:** Role-based access control for corporate teams.
- **Expense Management:** Tools for tracking and categorizing business spending.
- **B2B Payments:** Efficient and low-cost cross-border payments for businesses.

### 4.4. Platform Administration
- **Admin Dashboard:** A comprehensive backend interface for monitoring platform health, managing users, and generating regulatory reports.
- **Transaction Monitoring:** Real-time AML monitoring and alerting.
- **Compliance Tools:** Dashboards and reporting for regulatory compliance across all jurisdictions.
