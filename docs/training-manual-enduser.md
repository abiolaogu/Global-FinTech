# Training Manual — End User — Global FinTech Platform (AtlasX)
> Version: 1.0 | Last Updated: 2026-02-18 | Status: Draft
> Classification: Internal | Author: AIDD System

## Module 1: Getting Started (30 minutes)
- Downloading and installing the AtlasX app
- Creating your account
- Completing identity verification
- Setting up security (PIN, biometrics, MFA)

## Module 2: Managing Your Money (45 minutes)
- Understanding your dashboard
- Creating and managing currency wallets
- Viewing transaction history
- Setting spending budgets

## Module 3: Sending & Receiving (45 minutes)
- Peer-to-peer transfers
- International remittances
- Understanding FX rates and fees
- Scheduling recurring payments

## Module 4: Cards (30 minutes)
- Creating virtual cards for online shopping
- Ordering and activating physical cards
- Setting card limits and controls
- Freezing/unfreezing your card

## Module 5: Cryptocurrency (30 minutes)
- Buying your first crypto
- Understanding market prices
- Sending and receiving crypto
- Security best practices for digital assets

## Module 6: Business Features (45 minutes)
- Setting up a business account
- Managing team members and permissions
- Split payments for marketplace sellers
- Invoice and payment link generation

## Module 7: Security & Support (30 minutes)
- Protecting your account
- Recognizing phishing and scam attempts
- Contacting support
- Filing disputes
