# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

This document outlines the go-to-market strategy for the global fintech platform.

## 1. Regional Launch Sequencing

Our launch strategy is phased to prioritize regions with favorable regulatory environments and high market potential.

**PHASE 1 - Quarter 1 (Fast-track Regions)**
- **Regions:** Singapore, Estonia, Lithuania
- **Timeline:** 4-6 months
- **Target:** 50,000 users per region
- **Focus:** Establish a regulatory footprint and test the core MVP product.

**PHASE 2 - Quarter 2-3 (Secondary Regions)**
- **Regions:** Hong Kong, Switzerland, Ireland, Malta
- **Timeline:** 6-9 months per region
- **Target:** 100,000 users per region
- **Focus:** Expand into established fintech hubs and introduce multi-currency features.

**PHASE 3 - Quarter 4+ (Major Markets)**
- **Regions:** UK, Canada, EU core (France, Germany, Spain)
- **Timeline:** 8-15 months per region
- **Target:** 500,000+ users per region
- **Focus:** Scale user acquisition in large, competitive markets and launch crypto features.

**PHASE 4 - Year 2 (Emerging Markets)**
- **Regions:** USA, LATAM (Brazil, Mexico), Africa (Nigeria, Kenya), Asia (India)
- **Timeline:** 12-18 months per region
- **Focus:** Adapt the product for high-growth emerging markets.

## 2. Customer Acquisition Channels

We will employ a diversified customer acquisition strategy to achieve a target CAC of $15-25.

- **Paid Digital (40%):** Performance marketing campaigns on Meta (Facebook, Instagram), Google (Search, Display), and TikTok to drive initial user sign-ups.
- **Organic (30%):**
  - **Referral Program:** A viral loop with a K-factor target of 1.3x.
  - **SEO & Content Marketing:** Drive organic traffic through high-quality blog content, targeting keywords related to international money transfers, crypto trading, and digital banking.
  - **Community Building:** Engage with potential users on Reddit, Twitter, and Telegram.
- **Partnerships (20%):** Collaborate with banks, financial institutions, and large corporations to leverage our B2B2C offering.
- **PR & Content Marketing (10%):** Build brand authority and trust through media outreach and thought leadership content.

## 3. Pricing Strategy

Our pricing is designed to be transparent, competitive, and aligned with our multi-tiered service offering.

- **Domestic Transfers:** Free
- **International Transfers:** 0.5% - 2% fee + a small flat rate.
- **Forex Spread:** 0.5% - 1% on interbank rates.
- **Crypto Trading:** 0.5% - 2% per transaction.
- **Premium Subscription:** $10-20/month for premium features, lower fees, and higher limits.
- **Enterprise B2B:** Custom pricing for our white-label and API solutions.

## 4. Product Roadmap (18-Month View)

- **Months 1-3 (MVP Launch):** Basic wallet, fiat transfers, card issuance, and KYC in Phase 1 regions.
- **Months 4-6 (Multi-Currency & FX):** Launch multi-currency wallets, real-time FX, and business accounts.
- **Months 7-12 (Crypto & Advanced Features):** Integrate cryptocurrency wallets, trading, stablecoins, and lending/borrowing.
- **Months 13-18 (Scale & Enterprise):** Launch the white-label B2B2C solution and API marketplace, and expand into Phase 3 regions.
