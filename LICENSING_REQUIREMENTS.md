## European Union (EU)

### Required Licenses

#### 1. Electronic Money Institution (EMI) License
**Regulator:** National Competent Authority (e.g., Central Bank of Ireland, De Nederlandsche Bank)
**Directive:** E-Money Directive (2009/110/EC), PSD2 (2015/2366)

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** €350,000 (minimum)
- **Ongoing Capital:** 2% of average outstanding e-money OR €350,000 (whichever is higher)
- **Safeguarding:** 100% of customer funds segregated in safeguarding account
- **Governance:** Fit & Proper management (2+ directors)
- **Compliance:** AML/CFT, GDPR, operational resilience
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Issue e-money (digital wallets)
- Execute payment transactions
- Foreign exchange
- Payment initiation services (PIS)
- Account information services (AIS)

**Passporting:** EU EMI license allows operations across all EEA states without additional licensing.

---

#### 2. Crowdfunding Service Provider License
**Regulator:** ESMA (European Securities and Markets Authority)
**Regulation:** European Crowdfunding Service Providers Regulation (ECSPR)

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** €25,000
- **Ongoing Capital:** Higher of €25,000 or 25% of fixed overheads
- **Offering Limit:** €5 million per project (12 months)
- **Disclosure:** Key investment information sheet (KIIS)
- **Governance:** Complaints handling, conflict of interest policies
- **Timeline:** 3-6 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Loan-based crowdfunding (P2P lending)
- Investment-based crowdfunding
- Cross-border passporting across EU

**Key Obligations:**
- Individual investment limits (€1,000 or €5,000 depending on investor sophistication)
- Borrower credit assessment
- Default risk warnings

---

#### 3. MiFID II License (for Trading)
**Regulator:** National Competent Authority
**Directive:** Markets in Financial Instruments Directive II (2014/65/EU)

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** €50,000-€730,000 (depending on services)
- **Client Money Protection:** Segregated accounts
- **Best Execution:** Demonstrate best execution policies
- **Transaction Reporting:** Real-time reporting to regulators
- **Timeline:** 12-18 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Securities brokerage
- Portfolio management
- Investment advice

---

#### 4. Crypto Asset Service Provider (CASP)
**Regulator:** National Competent Authority
**Regulation:** Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA)

**Requirements:**
- **Authorization:** Required for custodying, exchanging, or operating trading platforms for crypto-assets
- **Capital Requirements:** €50,000-€150,000
- **Custody:** Cold storage, insurance requirements
- **Governance:** AML/CFT, consumer protection
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months (MiCA fully applies end of 2024)

## United Kingdom (UK)

### Required Licenses

#### 1. Electronic Money Institution (EMI) License
**Regulator:** Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** £350,000
- **Safeguarding:** 100% of customer funds
- **AML Registration:** Separate AML registration
- **Timeline:** 6-9 months
- **Application Fee:** £5,000

**Services Permitted:**
- E-money issuance
- Payment services
- FX services

**Post-Brexit:** No longer passports to EU (requires separate EU license).

---

#### 2. Consumer Credit License
**Regulator:** FCA
**Regulation:** Consumer Credit Act 1974

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** No specific minimum
- **Application Fee:** £1,000-£2,500
- **Fit & Proper Test:** Directors background checks
- **Timeline:** 4-6 months

**Services Permitted:**
- P2P lending platform operation
- Consumer credit intermediation

**Key Obligations:**
- Borrower affordability assessments
- Clear disclosure of APR
- Complaints handling procedures
- FCA reporting

---

#### 3. Investment Firm Authorization
**Regulator:** FCA
**Regulation:** FCA Handbook (IFPRU, COBS)

**Requirements:**
- **Initial Capital:** £50,000-£730,000
- **Client Money Rules (CASS):** Segregated accounts
- **Professional Indemnity Insurance:** Required
- **Timeline:** 9-15 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Execution of orders
- Portfolio management
- Investment advice

---

#### 4. Cryptoasset Registration
**Regulator:** FCA
**Regulation:** Money Laundering Regulations 2017

**Requirements:**
- **Registration:** No specific capital requirement
- **AML/CFT:** Robust AML procedures
- **Fee:** £2,000
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Cryptoasset exchange
- Custody wallet services

---

## United States (USA)

### Federal Level

#### 1. Money Services Business (MSB) Registration
**Regulator:** Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN)
**Regulation:** Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)

**Requirements:**
- **Registration:** Free (online via BSA E-Filing)
- **AML Program:** Written AML compliance program
- **SAR Filing:** Suspicious Activity Report procedures
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks

**Services Requiring MSB:**
- Money transmission
- Currency exchange
- Check cashing

---

### State Level

#### 2. Money Transmitter Licenses (50 States)
**Regulator:** State Banking Departments

**Requirements (varies by state):**
- **Bonds:** $25,000-$500,000 per state
- **Net Worth:** $25,000-$500,000
- **Background Checks:** Principals and control persons
- **Timeline:** 6-18 months (per state)
- **Nationwide Cost:** $1-3 million total

**Key States:**
- **New York (BitLicense for crypto):** $5,000 application fee, $500,000 bond
- **California:** $300,000 net worth, $250,000-$7M surety bond
- **Texas:** $300,000 net worth

**Alternative:** Use licensed MSB partner (e.g., Synapse, Evolve Bank) to avoid direct licensing.

---

#### 3. SEC Registration (for Securities/Trading)
**Regulator:** Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)

**Requirements:**
- **Broker-Dealer Registration:** Form BD
- **Net Capital Rule:** $25,000-$250,000
- **FINRA Membership:** Required for broker-dealers
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months
- **Costs:** $100,000+ (legal, compliance setup)

**Services:**
- Securities brokerage
- Investment advisory

---

#### 4. CFPB Compliance (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau)
**Regulation:** CFPB regulations for consumer lending

**Requirements for P2P Lending:**
- **Disclosure:** Truth in Lending Act (TILA), Regulation Z
- **Prohibited Practices:** UDAAP (Unfair, Deceptive, Abusive Acts or Practices)
- **Data Privacy:** Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA)

---

## Singapore

### Required Licenses

#### 1. Major Payment Institution (MPI) License
**Regulator:** Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS)
**Act:** Payment Services Act 2019

**Requirements:**
- **Base Capital:** SGD 250,000 (USD 185,000)
- **Paid-Up Capital:** SGD 1 million for account issuance
- **AML/CFT:** Compliance with PS Act
- **Timeline:** 6-9 months

**Services Permitted:**
- E-money issuance
- Cross-border money transfer
- Domestic money transfer

**Thresholds:**
- MPI: > SGD 3 million average monthly transaction volume OR > SGD 5 million average daily outstanding e-money

---

#### 2. Capital Markets Services (CMS) License
**Regulator:** MAS
**Act:** Securities and Futures Act (SFA)

**Requirements:**
- **Base Capital:** SGD 100,000-SGD 1 million (depending on services)
- **Risk-Based Capital:** Ongoing requirement
- **Representatives:** Licensed representatives (pass MAS exams)
- **Timeline:** 9-15 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Dealing in securities
- Fund management
- Securities advisory

---

#### 3. Digital Payment Token (DPT) License
**Regulator:** MAS
**Act:** Payment Services Act 2019

**Requirements:**
- **Base Capital:** SGD 250,000
- **Technology Risk:** Cybersecurity and technology risk management
- **AML/CFT:** Enhanced CDD for crypto
- **Timeline:** 6-12 months

**Services Permitted:**
- Cryptocurrency exchange
- Digital wallet custody
- Crypto payment facilitation

## Cross-Jurisdictional Strategy

### Recommended Approach

#### Phase 1: Establish EU Base (Year 1)
**Why:** EU passporting allows 27+ country access with single license.

1. **Lithuania EMI License** (fastest EU EMI, 6 months)
   - Cost: €50,000-€100,000 (legal/consulting)
   - Capital: €350,000
   - Passporting to all EEA

   - Cost: €30,000-€50,000
   - Capital: €25,000

#### Phase 2: UK Expansion (Year 1-2)
3. **UK EMI License**
   - Cost: £50,000-£80,000
   - Capital: £350,000

4. **UK Consumer Credit License** (P2P)
   - Cost: £15,000-£25,000

#### Phase 3: US Market (Year 2)
**Option A: Partner Model** (Lower cost, faster)
- Partner with licensed MSB (Synapse, Evolve Bank, Column)
- Avoid state-by-state licensing
- Time to market: 3-6 months

**Option B: Direct Licensing** (Higher cost, more control)
- MSB registration (Federal)
- 10-15 key states first (NY, CA, TX, FL, IL, etc.)
- Cost: $500,000-$1,000,000
- Timeline: 12-18 months

#### Phase 4: Singapore (Year 2-3)
5. **MAS MPI License**
   - Cost: SGD 150,000-SGD 250,000
   - Capital: SGD 1,000,000

## Key Compliance Requirements Across All Jurisdictions

### 1. Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Counter-Terrorist Financing (CFT)
- **Customer Due Diligence (CDD):** Identity verification for all customers
- **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD):** For high-risk customers (PEPs, high-value transactions)
- **Transaction Monitoring:** Automated systems to detect suspicious patterns
- **SAR Filing:** Report suspicious activities to FIUs
- **Record Keeping:** 5-7 years

### 2. Know Your Customer (KYC)
- **Identity Verification:** Government-issued ID + proof of address
- **Beneficial Ownership:** For corporate customers
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Refresh KYC data every 1-3 years

### 3. Data Protection
- **EU:** GDPR compliance (fines up to 4% of global revenue)
- **UK:** UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018
- **US:** GLBA, state privacy laws (CCPA in California)
- **Singapore:** Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)

### 4. Safeguarding / Trust Accounts
- **Requirement:** Customer funds segregated from operational funds
- **Frequency:** Daily reconciliation
- **Third-Party:** Safeguarding accounts at Tier 1 banks

### 5. Operational Resilience
- **BCP/DR:** Business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- **Cybersecurity:** SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 certifications
- **Incident Reporting:** Report material incidents to regulators within 24-72 hours

## Risk Mitigation: Partner Licensing Model

Instead of obtaining all licenses directly, AtlasX can partner with licensed entities:

### Payment Services
- **Stripe Connect** (US, EU): Payment processing
- **Modulr** (UK/EU): E-money and payment accounts
- **Railsr** (UK/EU): Embedded finance platform

### Banking-as-a-Service
- **Synapse** (US): Banking infrastructure
- **Evolve Bank** (US): BaaS provider
- **Railsbank** (UK/EU): Banking platform

### P2P Lending
- **Partner Platform Model:** License P2P platform in one jurisdiction, use local partners for others

### Benefits
- Faster time to market (3-6 months vs 12-24 months)
- Lower capital requirements ($50K vs $1-3M)
- Reduced compliance burden

### Risks
- Dependency on partner
- Revenue sharing (10-30% fees)
- Less control over customer experience
