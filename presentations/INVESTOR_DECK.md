## Slide 2: The Problem

**4 Billion People Face Financial Exclusion**

### Current Pain Points:
1. **High Remittance Costs**
   - Average fee: 6.25% (World Bank)
   - $50 billion wasted annually on fees
   - 2-7 day settlement times

2. **Limited Access to Credit**
   - 1.4B adults excluded from formal credit
   - Predatory lending rates (30-400% APR)
   - No credit history = no opportunity

3. **Payment Fragmentation**
   - 25+ different payment systems globally
   - No interoperability
   - Poor UX, multiple apps needed

4. **Lack of Savings Culture**
   - 50% of adults have no emergency savings
   - Traditional ROSCAs offline and risky
   - No trust in formal institutions

### The Cost:
- **$380B** lost to inefficient payments annually
- **$50B** in high remittance fees
- **$200B** in informal lending (unregulated)
- **2B** people use informal ROSCAs
